## 2. What is sold

The **Inbox Pro** subscription gives access, for its period of validity, to:

- publishing ads **without any limit on their number**, replacing the quota of
  ten ads a month that applies to personal accounts;
- a **verified badge** shown on your ads and your public profile;
- **higher placement in search results at equal relevance**: a professional ad
  never comes ahead of a more relevant ad, only ahead of an equally relevant
  one;
- **statistics** per ad: views, favourites and contacts received;
- **priority support**.

The subscription changes neither the posting rules nor moderation: ads from a
professional account are checked like any other, and can be refused on the same
grounds.

## 5. Activation and duration

**The subscription is activated only after the payment provider confirms
payment**, that confirmation being received and verified by our servers. Your
browser returning to the site after a payment is not a confirmation.

Until confirmation is received, your order stays pending and no benefit is
granted.

Once confirmed, the subscription period runs from the confirmation, for the
duration given in article 3.

If payment fails, no amount is charged and you are told by email. You can start
again whenever you wish.

## 6. Renewal

**There is no automatic charge.** The subscription does not renew itself:
renewing is up to you, from your subscription area.

A reminder email is sent to you **three days before expiry**. You can turn this
reminder off in your notification preferences.

If you renew before expiry, the new period is added to the current one: no paid
day is lost.

At expiry, if you have not renewed, your account becomes a personal account
again: the quota of ten ads a month applies once more and the verified badge
disappears. **Ads already online are not taken down**; they follow their normal
life cycle until they expire.

**Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid for.** You
keep the benefits of the subscription until that date. Cutting the service
immediately would mean taking back a service you have paid for.

No reason is asked of you.
