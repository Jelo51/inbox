## 6. Inbox's role

Inbox provides a publishing space and a messaging service. **Inbox is not a
party to transactions between its members**: it sells nothing, buys nothing,
holds no goods, collects no payment between buyer and seller, and arranges no
delivery.

The only service Inbox sells directly is the professional subscription,
governed by its [terms of sale]({{site.url}}/legal/conditions-de-vente).

Ads are checked before they go online. That check covers compliance with the
[posting rules]({{site.url}}/legal/regles-de-publication). It is neither an
appraisal of the item, nor a guarantee of its condition, authenticity, price,
or of the seller's honesty. It is no substitute for the care described in our
[safety advice]({{site.url}}/securite).

## 7. Reporting content

Every ad, every profile and every conversation has a report button. You can
also write to {{contact.abus}}.

Give the address of the page concerned and the reason for the report. Reports
are reviewed by our moderation team, which may remove content, suspend or ban
an account.

About conversations: Inbox messaging is end-to-end encrypted and **our teams
cannot read your messages**. Reporting a conversation therefore requires your
explicit agreement to send us the messages concerned, decrypted by your own
device. This is explained in the
[privacy policy]({{site.url}}/legal/confidentialite).
