## 1. Purpose

Inbox is an online classified ads service. It lets members post ads, browse
them, and contact each other through encrypted messaging.

The basic service is free. A paid professional subscription is offered
separately; it is governed by the
[terms of sale]({{site.url}}/legal/conditions-de-vente).

## 2. Who may register

Registration is open to people **aged 18 or over**. You declare this explicitly
when you register.

If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, it is closed and the associated
data deleted.

A valid email address is required. It must be confirmed before you can post an
ad or message a member: that is our first barrier against disposable accounts.

A Cameroonian phone number may be added. It stays optional, and is **never
shown in the clear**: other members see only a masked preview and must
explicitly ask to see it. Every viewing is recorded and their number is
limited.

## 4. Posting an ad

### Quota

A personal account may publish **ten ads per calendar month**. The counter
resets on the 1st of each month, Douala time. A professional subscription lifts
this limit.

An ad refused by moderation uses no quota.

### Content

You warrant that you have the right to sell what you offer, that your ad is
accurate, and that it complies with the
[posting rules]({{site.url}}/legal/regles-de-publication), which form part of
these terms.

Photographs must show the item actually offered. The metadata of your photos,
including GPS location, is automatically removed before publication: a photo
taken at your home must not broadcast your address.

### Duration

A published ad stays online for **60 days**. You can renew it during the week
before it expires. After that it expires and stops being visible, without being
deleted immediately.

### Editing

Changing the title, description or price of an ad that is online sends it back
to moderation. That is what prevents publishing a harmless ad and then changing
its content once approved.

## 5. Moderation

No ad goes online without being checked first by our team.

An automatic mechanism flags certain risky wordings and moves the ads concerned
to the front of the review queue. **That mechanism decides nothing**: every
publication and every refusal is a human decision.

A refusal always states its reason. The reason is sent to you by email, with
any note from the team. You can correct your ad and submit it again.

An ad already online can be taken down if a breach is found afterwards, in
particular following a report.

## 6. Encrypted messaging, and its limits

Messages exchanged on Inbox are **end-to-end encrypted**. They are encrypted on
your device before being sent, and can only be decrypted by the person you are
writing to.

What that means in practice:

- Our servers carry data they cannot open.
- We know the sender, the recipient, the ad concerned and the date of a
  message, but **never its content**.
- We cannot restore your messages if you lose your key.
- We cannot read a conversation to settle a dispute.

**Consequence for reports.** To report a conversation, you must agree that the
messages concerned be decrypted by your device and sent to our moderation team
in the clear. Nothing is sent without your explicit agreement, and the
interface tells you how many messages will be sent.

**Backing up your key.** You can back up your private key, encrypted with a
secret phrase known only to you, so as to restore it on another device. We keep
only the encrypted result. **If you lose that phrase, nobody — not even us —
can recover it**, and messages already received become permanently unreadable.

A conversation can only be opened about an ad. You can block a member at any
time; blocking works both ways.

Some data is kept despite deletion, because the law requires it: payments and

fraud, impersonation, harassment, circumventing a sanction or the quota.

Every suspension and every ban states its reason, which is sent to you. If you
believe it is a mistake, write to {{contact.general}}.

A professional subscription running at the time of a ban for serious breach
gives no right to a refund.
